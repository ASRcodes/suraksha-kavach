Suraksha Kavach dataset/model notices

URL model and bundled training-domain blocklist derive from PhiUSIIL by Arvind Prasad
and Shalini Chandra (2024), UCI 967, CC BY 4.0. Changes: URL-only features,
registrable-domain partitioning, filtering, model training and domain extraction.
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/967/phiusil-phishing-url-dataset
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

English text data includes SMS Spam Collection by Tiago Almeida and Jos Hidalgo,
UCI 228 (2011), CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.24432/C5CC84. Spam is a proxy, not verified fraud.

Synthetic text data: anmolshrivastav/scam_ham_india_14_languages and
sidzzz07/scamshield-dataset (Synthetic_Tier_C only), publisher-declared MIT.
Changes: normalization, URL removal from language features, grouping, training,
and held-out demo composition. Dataset revisions and checksums are in ml/reports.

Tranco: Victor Le Pochat, Tom Van Goethem, Samaneh Tajalizadehkhoob,
Maciej Korczynski and Wouter Joosen. Used for a reviewed allowlist and benign-domain
proxy; provider-specific licenses include CC BY 3.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 and CC BY-NC 4.0.
This release is a noncommercial hackathon/research build. https://tranco-list.eu/
